Terra Command turns Terraria's landscape into a Cortex Command style battlefield.

Start as your team's brain. A ship delivers two armed Coalition riflemen, a digger and grenades. Switch between bodies, protect the brain, buy equipment in orbit and destroy the enemy brain.

- Shared actor movement: mass, momentum, walking, prone crawling, jetpacks, impacts and ragdolls. Body swaps preserve physical state.
- Physical equipment: deliberate pickup, carried weapon queues, magazines, reloads, recoil and terrain-blocked muzzles.
- Coalition pistol, assault rifle, compact rifle and sniper rifle data ports, alongside original devices.
- Pixel terrain destruction, cargo ships, orbital stockpile, purchasing and unit orders.
- Conversion mode suppresses ambient Terraria spawns and vanilla starter tools for new characters. Existing characters and worlds are preserved.

This remains a partial conversion. Artwork is original; the full Cortex Command asset library and atom-based physics solver are not included.
