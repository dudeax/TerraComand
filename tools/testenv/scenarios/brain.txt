# The brain robot: the team's first body; slow, no jetpack; losing it is game over and the world is lost.
nospawn
clearnpcs
goto spawn
flatten 60 24
wait 30
claimbrain
expect kind brain
expect brainstate alive
give Revolver
give MusketBall 200
hold Revolver
aim 200 -40
wait 10
screenshot brain-robot
