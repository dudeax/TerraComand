# Limbs, hatches and ballistics — working 0.11.0

Living actors now track four removable limbs. Projectile wounds detach arms after eight hits and legs after ten. Missing limbs disappear from the native rig and contact solver, remove their mass, and remain missing when switching bodies or saving their physical state. A single remaining arm gives rifles their unsupported-fire spread penalty while still supporting one-handed pistols. Losing both legs forces a prone stance; losing both arms drops the held weapon and prevents handheld weapon use. Crabs retain their mounted weapon. Detached limbs become temporary physical NPCs with native sprite silhouettes, inherited translation/spin and terrain collisions. Their lifetime is one minute. Body identities keep delayed multiplayer injury messages from applying to a different controlled body.

Dropships carry the native left and right hatch sprites, pivots and collision masks. Doors swing 28 degrees over 250ms. Each hatch weighs 200kg and has the source 750 N·s joint strength. Joint overloads and explosions can detach doors into physical craft wrecks. A missing hatch leaves its side of the bay open; the remaining door still follows the ship's opening command. Hatch state and all six craft attachment slots synchronize.

Standard, silver, high-velocity, crystal, cursed, ichor and chlorophyte bullets now trace terrain in half-pixel steps. Source rifle/sniper/pistol round profiles supply mass and sharpness. A penetrating round pays the cell's integrity cost, slows down and continues with reduced damage. A stopped round spends its available work weakening that cell, so sustained fire keeps digging instead of reaching a permanent hard stop. Partial fracture work persists in world saves; carved holes use the terrain mask synchronization. Protected terrain and mining gates still stop rounds. CC weapons normalize Terraria extra updates so sniper rounds do not accidentally travel three times their configured speed. Idle riflemen calculate a low firing arc so their rounds reach distant targets. Supported rounds advance through our solver with CC gravity; Terraria movement and speed caps no longer determine their actual travel.

In multiplayer the server commits terrain work and bullet velocity. Clients predict travel, wait for the server when blocked, and accept authoritative continuation or termination. Projectile identity plus a spawn token protects against delayed corrections affecting reused projectile slots.

This remains an adapted solver. Terrain uses 2px cells. Flesh and armour penetration, exit wounds, bleeding, limb repair, detachable heads/jetpacks, full articulated constraints and collisions between moving bodies remain unfinished. NPC hit detection retains Terraria's broad hitbox with limb routing near the contact bones. Limb detachment currently uses projectile wound counts, rather than general joint stress from every collision. Detached pieces are temporary and do not persist across world reloads. Hatches have no artificial projectile wound limit; their source preset specifies joint strength instead.

Reference data is from the local CC Community checkout at `cca55f154ba49a17436cf91d91e1302fe45395db` (`DropshipMK1.ini`, Coalition infantry/device presets, `Atom::Travel`). Some limb masses and contact resolution remain adaptations to this conversion.

## Validation

The Release build succeeds with zero errors and four existing warnings. The standalone harness passes 143 checks, including limb mass/support, injury/body identity persistence, legacy saves, hatch separation/opening, controlled dropship landing, finite penetration work, protected terrain and ballistic aiming.

All 34 gameplay scenarios have passing coverage. The final full run had 409 passing checks and two failures: the scripted headshot still used straight-line aim, and the distant brain-hunt actors did not compensate for bullet drop. The fixture now accounts for the arc and lets sharp aim settle; the AI uses a tested low-arc calculation. Subsequent combat, remains, unit-orders and units-combat reruns pass all 55 checks. The actor fixture separately resets contact/gait state before crawling so a preceding ragdoll fall does not determine its starting stride phase.

Dedicated-server tests with two clients pass: 21 player-limb/body-swap checks; 22 hatch, mass, wreck and actual projectile-driven arm-severing checks; and 12 terrain/ballistic checks, including no leftover waiting bullets. Earlier targeted in-game tests also verify that a fast round emerges from a thin wall with lower speed and damage and continues flying. Temporary limbs and craft wrecks do not persist across reloads. The headless environment can still emit its existing native/file-log errors during teardown after the completed DONE results.
